美珍香 (Bee Cheng Hiang, the category giant) / 月餅 / 馬來西亞手信 / 豬肉乾 /
肉鬆, rotating 2/day. Two traps found on the first pull and coded in: HK's
肉乾/肉鬆 ad space is heavily PET TREATS (cat/dog exclusions added), and the

collab — which is exactly the gifting window a bak kwa relaunch would ride.
Baseline: 143 ads from 84 HK advertisers stored.

GOOGLE CALENDAR, WITHOUT OAUTH. /api/calendar serves an ICS feed of every open
dated next step plus each work project's target date. Google subscribes to the
URL; ticked-off steps drop out on its next refresh. A dedicated low-privilege
CALENDAR_FEED_TOKEN rides in the URL (calendar fetchers can't send headers) —
deliberately not CRON_SECRET, which guards endpoints that spend money. Fails
closed when unset. The honest trade, documented in the route: Google refreshes
subscribed calendars on its own schedule, hours not minutes — fine for
day-grained deadlines, wrong tool for anything finer.
