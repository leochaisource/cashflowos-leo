feat(bot+projects): give Jarvis ads tools, add two demo clients, fix loser ranking

THE BOT COULD NOT ANSWER AD QUESTIONS
All 13 of Jarvis's read tools query one table — `records`. Ad spend, CPL and
creative performance live in `ad_daily`, and the leads live in a Google Sheet, so
"what's my CPL" had no tool behind it. A bot instructed never to guess a number
correctly refused to answer. lib/bot-ads-tools.ts adds seven: list_projects,
get_ad_performance, winning_ads, losing_ads, project_funnel_status, leads_today,
and refresh_ads.

They read the morning snapshot rather than Meta, because a chat reply has to come
back in a second and a Graph call is 2-5s and rate-limited. refresh_ads is the
escape hatch — it calls Meta live when the owner asks for current numbers, and it
can only read: there is no path from the bot into a campaign or a budget.

TWO DEMO CLIENTS
Lotus Clinic Group and Kestrel Advisory have no ad account and no sheet. Their
numbers are seeded into the same three tables a real client fills from Meta, so
the dashboard, the 8am brief and every bot tool treat them identically — one
branch at the source (lib/demo.ts) instead of a demo mode threaded through the
app. They cost no Meta call and no Adyntel credit: their competitor section is
rebuilt from stored raw_payload, the same trick the offline replay tool uses.
Their shapes deliberately differ — one comfortably under target CPL, one over —
so a portfolio view shows contrast instead of three of the same.

LOSER RANKING WAS WRONG
"Bottom 3 by CPL" with three ads running includes the best ad in the account: the
same creative appeared as both the winner and a loser. A loser now has to be
losing relative to the account — no leads at all, or 25% worse than blended CPL —
and a winner can never appear in the list. CTR losers are judged against the
account's own click-through rate rather than an absolute floor.

Also: Nurture is not downstream of Closed, so the river no longer prints a
meaningless "150%" conversion into it, and a ratio that rounds to 0% now shows
"<1%" instead of reading as "nothing converts".
