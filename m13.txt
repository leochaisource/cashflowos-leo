feat(metrics): CPM, CTR, CPC everywhere — and yesterday against the last 3 days

The inputs were always in ad_daily (impressions, clicks, link clicks, spend).
Nothing ever divided them, so the app could report cost per lead but not the two
metrics that explain WHY the cost per lead moved.

- lib/delivery.ts: one delivery() used by the dashboard, the brief, the bot and
  the offline replay, so none of them can disagree. Free of `server-only` on
  purpose — that guard is what stopped the replay script sharing this maths.
- Campaign insights now request clicks, inline_link_clicks, reach, frequency,
  cpm, ctr and cpc, and prefer META'S computed cpm/ctr/cpc over re-deriving them,
  so the brief cannot drift from Ads Manager by a rounding. Meta returns ctr as a
  percentage; it is normalised to a fraction on the way in.
- accountInsights(): the live window read, and the only correct source of
  multi-day REACH and FREQUENCY. Daily reach is de-duplicated people and cannot
  be summed — so window-level frequency is deliberately absent from the stored
  path rather than quietly wrong. Per day, both are exact and are reported.
- The morning brief gains a DELIVERY block: yesterday, the trailing 3 days
  expressed per day, and the percentage change between them. "Past 3 days" means
  the three COMPLETE days behind us, because today is still being written and
  would drag every average down as the morning goes on. The prompt is told which
  direction is good for each metric — down for CPM/CPC/CPL, up for CTR.
- The bot gains ad_metrics_detail (yesterday / 3-day / window / per-day /
  per-ad, with live=true for Meta right now, including the full action list so
  "how many landing page views" needs no deploy), and get_ad_performance now
  carries CPM and CTR so the commonest follow-up costs no second call.
- Project pages show CPM, link CTR, CTR, CPC and click-to-lead, plus a
  yesterday-vs-3-day table coloured by whether each move is good or bad.

Verified against the live account: yesterday CPM RM19.27, link CTR 2.30%, CPL
RM6.58 against a 3-day average of RM20.37 / 2.60% / RM4.38 — the CPL rise came
with a CTR fall while CPM improved, which is the kind of read the numbers exist
to support.
