fix(telegram): brief destinations are additive — your chat AND the group

Pointing a client's brief at a group replaced the owner's copy instead of adding
to it, so setting up a client group would have quietly stopped Leo's own
briefing. Every destination now stacks: owner's chat, then the agency team list,
then that client's own groups, deduped with the owner first.

Verified across the cases that matter: with a group configured, Claude Malaysia
goes to both owner and group while Dianna and the demo clients still go to the
owner alone; an id listed twice sends once; a mistyped group id degrades to the
owner rather than sending nowhere.
