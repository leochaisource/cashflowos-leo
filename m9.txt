feat(bot): answer "who is this person", tag receipts to a client, show project costs

Three things the owner asked the bot for and it could not do.

1. A NAME ON ITS OWN. lookup_person searches the pipeline, the customer list,
   the invoices AND the live leads sheet at once, and returns the stage, how warm
   they look WITH the evidence for it, when to follow up, which ad brought them,
   and every unpaid invoice with its due date and days overdue. Money that is
   late outranks a written next action, because chasing it IS the next action.
   Verified against real rows: a paid lead reads "customer / has paid" with the
   ad that produced them; a client with a 5-day-old invoice reads "today — chase
   RM 2,200, 5 day(s) overdue"; an unknown name says so instead of guessing.

2. RECEIPTS NOW BELONG TO A CLIENT. The photo caption picks the project
   ("Lotus Clinic — venue deposit"), matched by lib/ad-clients.matchProject and
   carried through the payload into meta.project, so the same tag lands whether
   it arrived by photo, by typed expense, or by hand. An ambiguous caption ASKS
   rather than picking — a receipt on the wrong client's P&L is worse than one
   that needs sorting later — and an untagged file says so and shows the caption
   format. Proven end to end: a test receipt filed through the real executor
   came back with meta.project set, and was cleaned up.

3. THE TAG NOW HAS A PAYOFF. Project pages show the receipts filed against that
   client, the true total cost (media plus everything else) and net against cash
   collected. Before this you could tag a receipt and watch nothing happen.

Also fixes the person heuristic ignoring customer rows, which reported a client
with RM 3,970 outstanding as "interest: unknown, no activity recorded".
