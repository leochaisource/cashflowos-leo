feat(ads-brief): real competitor data for the two demo clients

They had keywords but never used them: the demo branch skipped Adyntel entirely
and rebuilt their market from ads COPIED off the real clients — so an aesthetics
clinic's brief discussed AI workshop ads. A demo project has no ad account, but
its market is real, and Adyntel does not care that our side of the account is
seeded.

- Both clients get proper keyword sets (5 each, rotating 2/day = 2 credits/day)
  plus relevance and exclusion terms. Result: Lotus 72% on-topic, Kestrel 41%,
  against 38% on the untuned AI keyword set.
- Exclusions now also read the advertiser's PAGE NAME, because that is often the
  only place the disqualifying fact appears: country_code=MY still returns
  "ReDerm Clinic Bangkok" and "Karlovy clinic thrissur", whose copy never
  mentions where they are. Relevance groups deliberately still ignore the page
  name — a page called "AI Academy" must not satisfy "sells training" on its
  name alone.
- Every project now searches for real competitors; the stored-ads rebuild is a
  FALLBACK for demo clients only when the search fails. A real client should see
  that their feed broke, not a quietly recycled market from last week.
- scripts/adyntel-seed-client.ts: populate one client's whole keyword list on day
  one, since "new since last fetch" is meaningless without a baseline. Prints the
  credit bill before spending and degrades if the enrich columns are missing.
- The replay tool now handles demo clients (ad_daily + project_funnel), so a dry
  run reads like the real 8am message for all four projects.

VERIFIED, and it settles an open question: Adyntel IGNORES the undocumented
filters it echoes. Two identical searches — one plain, one sending
active_status/search_type/start_min_date — returned the same 30 ads, with the
echo showing the server's own defaults both times. The echo describes what
Adyntel did, not what we asked for. Noted in the config so nobody chases it again.

Seeded: Lotus 122 ads from 72 advertisers, Kestrel 135 from 91. Real Malaysian
clinics and training providers — Borneo Medispa (987 days), Dr. Chong Clinic,
Maxwell Leadership Asia.
