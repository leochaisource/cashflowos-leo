feat(dashboard): today alongside yesterday and the 3-day average

The comparison table gains a "Today so far" column, and the bot's metrics tools
gain the same block.

The care is in what today ISN'T. A partial day's TOTALS are not comparable with a
full day — at 10am "spend RM71 vs RM92 yesterday" reads as a collapse when it is
simply an unfinished day. Its RATES are perfectly comparable, because CPM, CTR,
CPC and CPL are per-impression or per-click and do not care what time it is.

So spend and leads are greyed and marked ⏳ as running totals, the percentage
change column still compares yesterday against the 3-day average (two complete
days), and a caption says today covers everything up to the last sync with a
pointer to Refresh. The bot's tool output carries the same caveat in words, with
an explicit instruction never to report spend or leads as having "dropped" based
on today.

Live right now: today RM71.52 at CPM RM19.94, link CTR 2.79%, CPL RM3.58 against
yesterday's RM92.16 / RM25.11 / 2.81% / RM5.12 — cheaper media and a better cost
per lead, on a day that is only part-run.
