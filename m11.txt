fix(cron): run the morning briefs two at a time

Fully sequential was right at two clients and stops being right at four. Each
client is several Adyntel calls plus a model write-up — 40-70 seconds — and four
in a row runs at the 300s function ceiling, where the LAST client's brief simply
never sends and nothing says why. All-at-once would instead risk rate limits.

Pairs halve the wall time and keep concurrent load roughly where it already was
(one client already fires 8 parallel Adyntel searches). A failing client still
cannot take down the others.
